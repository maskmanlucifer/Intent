Pin, prioritize, and reach us faster

Three changes aimed at keeping the important stuff visible and making it easier to talk to us.

Pin your most important links

Every link in Linkboard now has a pin action. Pinned links float to the top of the board, so the ones you reach for daily never get buried under new saves.

Prioritize tasks with P1 / P2 / P3

Flag any task as P1, P2, or P3 straight from the task toolbar. Each priority gets its own view in the sidebar, pulling matching tasks in from every folder into one place.

"Help us improve" no longer routes through a form you fill in blind. One click opens your email client, addressed to us, so you know exactly where your words are headed.

Improved Task ↔ Subtask Navigation

Navigate seamlessly between tasks and subtasks using Shift+Enter. On a task, jump to the last subtask or create a new one. On a subtask, move to the next task or create one. Work faster with improved keyboard navigation.
